Before you shop

Common questions

Why do some products say “Ask for price”?

The current price is listed for some products. For others, contact the store to confirm price and availability.

Does an order request charge me?

No online payment is collected when you submit a request. The store confirms availability and price with you.

Do I need to sign in to order?

Yes. Continue with Google, save your delivery details, and submit the request from your account.

Where can I see my order requests?

Open your account and choose Orders. Updates from the store appear there.

Can I cancel a request?

You can cancel a pending request from Orders. If the store cancels it as out of stock, the status appears in your account.

Can I view more product photos?

Open a product card. When more photos are available, use the gallery controls on its detail page.
