Privacy

This page explains the information used by the Lootbox website when you browse, save your details or send an order request.

Information you provide

When you sign in and save a delivery profile, the site stores your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. An order request also records its items, delivery details, status and submission time so it can appear in your account and in the private seller dashboard.

Sign-in and order handling

Customer sign-in uses Google through Supabase Authentication. Supabase stores the account profile and order requests. The seller dashboard is restricted to the store's authorized seller accounts so the store can review requests and contact customers about availability, condition and price.

Cart and browsing preferences

The site uses browser storage to keep cart and preference information on your device. If you sign in, the cart may also be saved to your account so you can continue later.

Product reviews

If you submit a product review or comment, it may be shown with that product so other visitors can read it. Do not include private contact or address details in a public comment.

Questions

For questions about information connected to your Lootbox account or order, contact the store through Lootbox Store on Instagram.
