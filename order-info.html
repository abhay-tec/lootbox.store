BEFORE YOU ORDER

Order information

Please read the product listing and photos before sending an order request. Availability, product condition and price are confirmed by the store.

Order requests

Submitting a request saves it to your account and sends it to the store's private seller dashboard. A request is not a completed purchase. The store contacts you to confirm availability, condition and final price before proceeding.

Prices and availability

Prices and stock can change. If a listing says ?Ask for price,? contact the store to check the current price. A seller may cancel a request when an item is out of stock; the status will appear in your account.

Cancellation

You can cancel a pending order request from the Orders area in your account. Once the request is no longer pending, use the account order details to check its status or contact the store.

Payment, delivery and returns

The website does not collect payment when you submit an order request. Payment, delivery arrangements and any return terms must be confirmed directly with the store before you complete a purchase.
